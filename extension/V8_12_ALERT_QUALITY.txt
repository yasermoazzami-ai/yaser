v8.12 Alert Quality

- Volume spikes use interval activity, not cumulative-volume percentage.
- No volume alert from a tiny first print after inactivity.
- Queue/near-queue symbols require much larger value and trade count.
- Money/power alerts require meaningful fresh traded value.
- Crosses require 3 distinct active samples over >=4s plus hysteresis.
- Big-money trend changes require >=6 history points, >=15s span, stable direction, larger adaptive move, and fresh activity.
- Big-money formula and UI layout are unchanged.
